Our Story

Why I Built ECHO

💙

My mom had heart surgery. When medication was administered through her neck, the pressure was so intense she couldn't show her pain or express what she was feeling. I watched her struggle, unable to help.

My dad had Parkinson's disease. His mind was perfect—brilliant, sharp, full of thoughts—but he couldn't speak. He was trapped inside his own body. Those were very hard times for our entire family.

My dad is no longer with us. I created ECHO for them—so other families can give their loved ones the best quality of life they deserve, in their most difficult moments.

— Mirza, Founder of ECHO
