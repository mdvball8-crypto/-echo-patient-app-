Our Story

Why I Built ECHO

💙

My mom had heart surgery. When medication was administered through her neck, the pressure was so intense she couldn't show her pain or express what she was feeling. I watched her struggle, unable to help.

My dad had Parkinson's disease. His mind was perfect—brilliant, sharp, full of thoughts—but he couldn't speak. He was trapped inside his own body. Those were very hard times for our entire family.

My dad is no longer with us. I created ECHO for them—so other families can give their loved ones the best quality of life they deserve, in their most difficult moments.

— Mirza, Founder of ECHO

Features

Everything Your Loved One Needs

Designed with care for patients who can't speak, and the families who love them.

🧍

Interactive Body Map

Tap exactly where it hurts—external body AND internal organs. Rate pain 1-10 with color-coded intensity and specify the type of sensation.

🚨

One-Tap Alerts

12 large, fingertip-sized buttons with sound feedback. Emergency, pain, thirsty, hungry, bathroom, and more—no typing needed.

🎤

Voice Recognition

Hands-free communication. Simply say "pain," "help," or "thirsty" and ECHO responds. Perfect for patients with limited mobility.

💊

Medication Tracking

Log medications with dose, method (oral, IV, injection, topical), and who administered. Complete history with timestamps.

👥

Visitor Log

Track who enters and exits the room. Complete accountability with timestamps, visitor types, and roles for better care coordination.

📷

Document Capture

Take photos of medical documents, prescriptions, or anything important. Store and access them right within the app.

📊

Caregiver Dashboard

Real-time patient status (Stable, Needs Attention, Critical), activity log, medication history, and visitor tracking at a glance.

🖨️

Print & Export

Download complete reports as JSON or print professional summaries for doctor appointments and shift changes.

🦥

Kids Mode

Fun colors, friendly sloth mascot, child-appropriate icons. Makes communication less scary for children in hospital settings.

🔊

Full Accessibility

Sound feedback beeps and voice announcements for blind/low-vision users. Every action speaks so no one is left out.

🌐

Bilingual Support

Complete English and Spanish support. One-tap language switching for diverse families and care teams.

🛋️

Comfort Controls

Bed adjustments, bathroom assistance, sleep preferences, lighting controls—all the everyday needs at their fingertips.
